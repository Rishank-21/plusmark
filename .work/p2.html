Description

The Metallic Premium White Board uses high-quality, durable materials with a High Gloss Marker Grade HPL Sheet. Heavy-duty framing with Signature Dual-Tone Corners gives it a solid, professional build.

The ultra-smooth, high-gloss surface is made for effortless writing and easy erasing. It suits high-end offices, educational institutions and professional environments, and is extremely long-lasting and highly resistant to wear and tear.

Standard sizes on this page are 3 x 4 ft, 4 x 4 ft, 5 x 4 ft, 6 x 4 ft, 8 x 4 ft. Larger or non-standard sizes are made to order — send the size you need and the team will quote it.

Prices depend on series, size and quantity, so Plusmark confirms the figure for your exact requirement instead of publishing a list price. There is no minimum order — a single board can go straight in the cart.

Care & Delivery

Care and maintenance

  • Wipe the writing surface with a dry eraser or duster after use, then a soft cloth if any ghosting remains.
  • Use a slightly damp cloth on chalk surfaces and let the board dry before writing again.
  • Avoid solvent-based or abrasive cleaners, and do not leave marker ink on the surface overnight.
  • Do not use a writing board as a pin surface.

Delivery and installation

  • Boards are made to order after the size, surface and price are confirmed.
